## Why we started

HGSE brings together students from many countries, professions, and backgrounds, yet conversations often stay within familiar circles. Having grown up in China, studied in the UK, and now living in the US, I know how much a single good conversation can change how you see a place. We started Voices & Co- to create a space where stories cross communities, and where listening becomes a way to connect.

## Designing the format

We treated the community like a product and designed three formats, each inviting a different depth of engagement. Street interviews capture many quick, spontaneous voices; podcasts go deep with one guest at a time; and expert conversations bring in perspectives from beyond campus. Social events then turn listeners into participants, so the audience can become part of the next story.

Our content runs as three series: Co-Wonder for big questions, Co-Explore for discovering stories, and Co-Think for ideas in dialogue.

## A visual identity, built with AI

With a small team and weekly events, we needed design that was fast without being inconsistent. I created all of our event posters by vibe coding with ChatGPT inside a consistent design system of colors, type, and layout, so every poster is instantly recognizable as ours. I designed with accessibility in mind, keeping text legible and contrast strong so information reads clearly at a glance, whether on a screen or a hallway wall.

![Co-Wonder series poster with the tagline 'Big questions, open minds' over a lavender question mark](/images/bbG29oDSdU1USnkzRprCrB93jWo.webp)

## Early traction and what's next

In our first month, our five-person team produced two podcast episodes that reached 100+ listeners on campus. As co-founder, I lead recruitment, content production, outreach, and team operations. Running interviews has also sharpened a skill I use as a designer: asking open questions and listening for what people actually mean.

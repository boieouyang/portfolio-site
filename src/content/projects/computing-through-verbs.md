## The brief

Every week I take one verb through the same four stages. I write to define it, then research it through seven visual elements: space, line, shape, color, value, form, and texture. From there I sketch the strongest directions and build them as interactive prototypes, using Figma for visual exploration and Figma Make to bring motion and interaction to life.

## What's next

Learning is full of verbs too: explore, compare, reflect. This practice is shaping how I design interactions that invite learners to act, not just read.
